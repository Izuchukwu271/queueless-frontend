MY APPROACH

Learn by building.
Improve with every version.

I'm developing my skills by working on practical software projects and learning how to take them from an idea to a usable application. QueueLess is one of those projects: a queue-management experience built around business access, customer queue pages, and QR codes.

My goal is to keep learning, make deliberate improvements, and build software that solves real problems. This site will grow alongside the product and my work.
